D-ISLAND PRIVACY STATEMENT
What D-Island reads, where it goes, and what is stored

D-Island by h7 (ibuildrigs) - https://ibuildrigs.com
Applies to D-Island version 1.2.1.


IN SHORT

- D-Island has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no tracking.
- Almost everything it reads stays on your computer, in memory, only to show it in the island.
- It only contacts the internet for weather (and your town's name), for calendar links you add, to check for updates on GitHub, for Apple Music artwork (only if you switch that on), and once to download Microsoft's packaging tools (only if you choose the notifications setup).
- You can turn every feature below off in Settings, and you can delete all stored data (see section 6).


1. INFORMATION PROCESSED ON YOUR COMPUTER ONLY

Read to show it in the island, kept in memory, and not sent anywhere.

- Media: title, artist, album, artwork and playback position from apps that report to Windows' media controls (Spotify, Apple Music, browsers and others).
- Notifications: other apps' notifications in Windows' notification center (app name, icon, title and text). Only after you complete the one-time setup and Windows grants access. You can hide message text in Settings.
- Calendar: events from calendars added to Windows (after the one-time setup) and from iCal links you add: titles, times, locations and meeting links.
- Clipboard: when you copy something, the type of content and the first line of text, to show the "Copied" alert. Not stored. Content that password managers mark as private is never read. The text preview can be switched off.
- Keyboard: only whether Caps Lock or Num Lock changed, and whether the paste shortcuts (Ctrl+V, Shift+Insert) and the hide/show shortcut were pressed (which needs knowing whether Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Windows is held). No other keys are read and nothing you type in other apps is recorded.
- To-do list and notes: what you type on the island's To-Do and Notes pages. Stored only on this PC (see section 3) and never sent anywhere.
- Microphone and camera: only WHICH apps are currently using them, to show the privacy dot. D-Island never accesses your microphone or camera itself and never hears or sees anything.
- Audio level: the loudness of what is playing, to animate the music bars. No audio is recorded.
- Screen behind the island: for the Liquid Glass effect, Windows draws (or D-Island copies, in memory) the small area of the screen behind the island to blur it. It is never saved or sent. Window positions and types are checked to know what is behind the island.
- Devices and status: connected devices and their battery level, battery and charging state, Wi-Fi network name, VPN and internet status, Do Not Disturb, Energy Saver, volume and brightness.
- Files on the shelf: the locations of files you drag onto the island. The files themselves are not copied, read or uploaded.
- Apps: the list of apps in your Start menu, so you can choose quick-launch apps.


2. INFORMATION SENT OVER THE INTERNET

- Weather (Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com): your approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, or the city you chose, to get the forecast, every 20 minutes while the island runs; and city names you type in the search box. Windows provides the location when you choose "My location" and allow location access.
- Place name (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org), only with "My location": the same rounded location, to show your town's name on the weather page. Asked once per place; the name is remembered in your settings file.
- Calendar links (your calendar provider, such as Google, Microsoft or Apple): D-Island downloads the iCal links you add, about every 10 minutes. These links act like a password; D-Island stores them only in your settings file and shows only the website name.
- Apple Music motion artwork (Apple), OFF by default: if you switch it on, the artist and album of what you are playing are sent to Apple's iTunes Search and Apple Music web services to find and download the album's animated artwork.
- Updates (GitHub, https://github.com): once a day, D-Island asks GitHub for the latest official release of D-Island. The request contains only D-Island's version number. If there is a newer version, D-Island downloads it from GitHub, checks it against its published checksum, and installs it. You can turn checking and automatic installing off in Settings > Updates.
- Notifications setup (Microsoft NuGet, https://www.nuget.org), only if you run the setup: a one-time download of Microsoft's packaging tools.

These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request, and handle data under their own privacy policies. D-Island sends nothing else, and nothing at all to h7 / ibuildrigs.


3. WHAT IS STORED, AND WHERE

- %AppData%\DIsland\settings.json: your settings, including your chosen city, iCal links, quick apps and alarms.
- %AppData%\DIsland\error.log: technical error details if something goes wrong (no personal content is written on purpose).
- %LocalAppData%\DIsland\shelf.json: the file locations on your shelf.
- %LocalAppData%\DIsland\notes.json: your to-do list and notes.
- %LocalAppData%\DIsland\MotionArt\: cached Apple Music artwork videos (only if switched on), up to 200 MB.
- %LocalAppData%\DIsland\Identity\: packaging tools and the setup log (only if you ran the setup).
- %LocalAppData%\DIsland\Updates\: the latest downloaded update installer, until it's replaced by the next one.

Nothing leaves your computer from these files.


4. WINDOWS PERMISSIONS D-ISLAND MAY ASK FOR

Location (for weather); notification access and calendar access (after the one-time setup); and administrator permission once, to trust D-Island's own signing certificate (part of the same setup). You can withdraw them at any time in Windows Settings > Privacy & security, or by choosing "A city" for weather and switching notifications and the Windows calendar off in D-Island's Settings.


5. CHILDREN

D-Island does not knowingly collect any information from anyone; it has no online service.


6. DELETING YOUR DATA

Uninstall D-Island from Windows Settings > Apps and answer Yes when asked whether to delete your settings and saved data, or delete the folders listed in section 3. Uninstalling also removes the notifications setup.
