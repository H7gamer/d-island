How to use D-Island

A Dynamic Island for Windows. It sits at the top centre of your screen and shows what's going on: the time and weather when nothing is, and your music, timers and alerts when something is.

Open and close it

  • Hover over the island, or click it, to open it. Move the pointer away to close it.
  • Right-click the island to open Settings. You can also double-click the D-Island icon in the taskbar's notification area, or use D-Island Settings in the Start menu.
  • Press Ctrl + Alt + I to hide or show the island from anywhere.

Pages

Scroll with the mouse wheel or touchpad over the open island, or click the icons along its top, to switch pages. Scrolling a long list stops at its end; scroll again to move on.

HomeWhat's playing (Spotify, Apple Music, browsers and more), quick timers and a stopwatch.
NotificationsOther apps' notifications. Click one to open its app, ✕ to dismiss it.
CalendarThis month and your next events, with a Join button for Teams, Zoom, Meet and Webex.
ShelfDrag files onto the island to keep them handy, then drag them out wherever you need them.
WeatherNow, the next hours and the next five days.
AppsYour favourite apps, one click away. Choose them in Settings.

Alerts

The island briefly shows volume and brightness, battery and charging, devices connecting (with earbud battery), Caps Lock, copied text, Do Not Disturb, Wi-Fi and more.

  • An orange or green dot beside the island means an app is using your microphone or camera.
  • When two things run at once, for example a timer and music, the second appears as a small bubble beside the island.
  • Hover or click while an alert is showing to open its page.

Notifications and your Windows calendar

Windows only shares these with apps that complete a one-time setup. Choose it while installing, or later in Settings > Notifications. Windows asks for permission once.

Calendars from Google, Outlook or iCloud can also be added as a link in Settings > Calendar, with no setup.

Make it yours

Settings has the shape (island or notch), the material (including Liquid Glass), size, colours, fonts, alarms, and which alerts and pages to show. Everything applies straight away.
